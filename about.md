<h2 class="main-header">About</h2>
<p class="main-deets">
TFinAI is a trade-name of The Future is now AI, LLC, founded in November 2024 by <a href="https://sethbriney.com/">Seth Briney</a>, a double master's graduate of Western Washington University in mathematics and computer science. TFinAI was created to help launch individuals, organizations, and businesses into the technological future through advanced engineering, artificial intelligence, automation, cybersecurity, and creative systems design.

We believe the future should not be passively awaited, but actively engineered. Our mission is to accelerate innovation responsibly and ethically, empowering people with intelligent tools, resilient infrastructure, and transformative technologies that expand human capability rather than replace it. We are especially passionate about ethical AI, privacy-conscious systems, scientific thinking, and unconventional solutions to difficult technical problems.

Our approach is rooted in science, mathematics, and systems engineering. We combine creativity with rigor, experimentation with discipline, and futuristic thinking with practical execution. We pride ourselves on building systems that are reliable, efficient, secure, and thoughtfully designed — with the goal of helping our clients adapt, evolve, and thrive in an increasingly technological world.
