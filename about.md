<h2 class="main-header">About</h2>

<p class="main-deets">
TFinAI is a trade-name of The Future is now AI, LLC — a Washington professional scientific and technical services company founded in 2024 by <a href="https://sethbriney.com/">Seth Briney</a>.
<br><br>

TFinAI was built around a future-oriented vision of technology: intelligent systems, adaptive infrastructure, secure communications, automation, and advanced computing engineered not merely to keep pace with technological change, but to move ahead of it decisively.
<br><br>

We believe the organizations that thrive in the coming decades will be those that adapt early, automate intelligently, engineer resilient systems, and integrate AI strategically rather than reactively. Our mission is to help launch individuals and businesses into that future through advanced engineering, scientific thinking, and aggressive technological innovation.
</p>
